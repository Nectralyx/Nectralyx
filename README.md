# Nectralyx by Morgan Keay

My name is Morgan Keay.

I am a high school student with hopes to improve the world. I enjoy all areas of science, but specialize in mathematics and computer science.

I have experience with Python, Javascript, Swift, HTML/CSS, PHP, SQL, and ARM64 Assembly, but work mostly in C++ for its versatility and low-level optimization ability. I have made many personal projects, including games, apps, backend servers which connect to apps, and websites. However, my primary work since 2024 has been in machine learning. During my grade 10 year, I developed TensorKit, a tensor framework for Swift. The goal was primarily educational, and during its development I have gained a very deep understanding of deep learning. However, seeing as how DL projects are so computationally expensive, I have begun looking for a more efficient way to create AI. This has led me to where I am today. 

Now, I am working on an alternative to deep learning with efficiency in mind. I believe that hyperdimensional computing has great potential, and I hope to bring it further with my work.
